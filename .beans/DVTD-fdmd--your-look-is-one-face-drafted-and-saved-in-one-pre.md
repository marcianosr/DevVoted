**What:** The appearance tab shows one face with your owned borders and titles under it, and a single press saves the look. Borders and titles get their own tabs to browse and buy.

**Why:** Three previews said the same thing three times, and every press saved on the spot, so you could not settle on a look before others saw it.

## Done when
- [x] The appearance tab shows one face, your owned borders and your earned titles, and a save press
- [ ] Picking a border or title changes only the face until you save
- [x] Borders have their own tab where a locked border is tried on, then bought
- [x] Titles have their own tab to see progress
- [x] The ADR, wiki and changelog describe it
