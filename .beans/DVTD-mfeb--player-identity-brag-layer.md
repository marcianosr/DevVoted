Social signalling layer for DevVoted. Players should be able to display their specialisation, rank, and achievements to others — like League of Legends borders/titles on the loading screen, or F-Zero 99 player cards. The idea: a decoratable dev profile card showing your tier, pipeline style, categories mastered, streaks. Something worth screenshot-sharing. Distinct from a stats page — this is about identity and showing off, not analytics.
