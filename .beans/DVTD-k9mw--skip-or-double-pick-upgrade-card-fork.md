After passing a gate, give the player a fork instead of always offering 1 upgrade card:

- **Skip** — take no upgrade card. No pipeline complexity added, no reward. Pure breathing room ("air").
- **Standard** — 1 pick from 3 options (current behaviour).
- **Double pick** — choose 2 upgrade cards. Pipeline gets harder (2 new slots added), but unlocks the slot removal reward on the next gate pass (see DVTD-j2tn).

The skip/double-pick tension is the core strategic choice: sacrifice growth for relief, or take on extra risk for a unique reward.
