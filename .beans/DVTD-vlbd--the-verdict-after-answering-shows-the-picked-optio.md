**Why:** The reasons per answer land on the review and the poll page first; the moment of the verdict is where a wrong pick stings and the reason would teach most.

## Done when

- [ ] A wrong pick's verdict shows that option's "Why it’s wrong" when one is written
- [ ] A right pick's verdict shows its "Why it’s right"
- [ ] The hold still clears on its own timing and reads on a phone

## Notes

Follows "Every answer explains itself". The poll screen's `hint` today is the poll-level explanation.
