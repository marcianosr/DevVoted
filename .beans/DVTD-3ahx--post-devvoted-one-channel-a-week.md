**What:** One post a week in one channel from the growth doc, with its visitors, guests, sign-ins and seven-day return logged next to it.

**Why:** Fifty daily players is the sum of many small cohorts, and a post that is not logged teaches nothing about the next one.

## Done when
- [ ] The first stranger-facing post waits for the landing page and guest play
- [ ] Every post has a log row within a week of posting
- [ ] Twenty posts are logged
