Veteran QoL from the 2026-08-10 onboarding discussion: let a player save an assembled build as a personal preset, rendered as another row beside the official starter stacks. Absorbs collection growth without multiplying official bundles. Needs account-level persistence (same wave as the intro flags from ADR-026). Naming resolved 2026-08-10: the starter-bundle concept is now called a 'stack' throughout (ADR-026), not 'pack' — no collision with a future paid-pack monetization idea.
