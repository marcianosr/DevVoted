Community poll submission is live, but at scale there needs to be a clear moderation process. Define and implement: who reviews submissions, what the approval flow looks like (admin UI already exists?), quality criteria, and how fast new polls make it into the pool. Without this, content freshness degrades fast once real users are submitting.
