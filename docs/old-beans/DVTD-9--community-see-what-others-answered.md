Expand community visibility so players feel connected to others. Potential stat types to show:

- **What others answered**: per-option breakdown of how the community voted (correct vs incorrect)
- **Correct/incorrect ratio**: % of players who got this question right globally or per run type
- **Hardest questions**: which polls trip up the most players
- **Category performance**: how the community performs per category (e.g. "most players struggle with DevOps")
- **Streaks leaderboard**: who has the longest current or all-time streak
- **Daily participation**: how many players answered today's poll
- **Agreement rate**: how often your answer matches the majority

Ties in closely with DVTD-8 (game flow rethink) — placement and priority of these stats on the results page needs to be decided there.
