Brainstorm how to deepen the social layer of DevVoted. Goal: connect players with each other through cooperation, competition, or sabotage mechanics.

Ideas to explore:
- Help others (e.g. share a hint, gift storage)
- Egg each other on (taunts, reactions to others' answers, streaks)
- Thwart (e.g. a config that affects another player's shop or weights)
- Team/guild dynamics
- Reactions to leaderboard events
- Social feeds or activity logs
